ScribeIQ Complete

Side Project

A private, on-device call recorder and transcriber for macOS. Record a call from Zoom, Teams, a browser, or any other app and get a speaker-labeled transcript with no cloud services and no audio leaving the machine. A native Swift helper records the microphone and the call as two separate, synced tracks, so it always knows which words are yours; Whisper transcribes each track on Apple Silicon, and pyannote separates speakers on group calls. A React interface talks to a local FastAPI server over REST and a WebSocket for live level meters, timers, and job progress.

Technologies Used

Python, FastAPI, WebSockets, React, TypeScript, Vite, Vitest, Swift, ScreenCaptureKit, MLX Whisper, pyannote.audio, pywebview

Family Passport Map LIVE · IMPROVING v2.3.0

Side Project

A family-friendly web app for tracking which places you and your family have visited: all 50 US states on an interactive US map, and 195 countries on a world map you explore one continent at a time. Each family member gets their own color and profile picture, with visits tracked independently, "n/50 states" and "n/195 countries" counters, a side-by-side Compare view, and a List view showing who has been where. Microsoft sign-in keeps each account's People and photos private. Installable as a PWA on phone, tablet, and desktop.

Live on Azure App Service. Next up: Link and Join, so families can share and manage People together.
