The lookup tool

When the Inventory Control & Quality Assurance team was set up, I was one of its first members. Our job is to find out why an inventory error happened, not just fix the count. That meant digging through history in the inventory management system, one screen at a time.

With two colleagues, I built an Excel tool to do that digging. I wrote the SQL and most of the VBA. You type a team member, an item, a location, a pallet or another key into input cells; the tool runs a parameterized query against the system's database and a macro lays the results out on a second sheet. Management approved it for the team, and managers use it a couple dozen times a day too.

I keep system names and internal details off this site. I'm happy to talk through how it works in an interview.
